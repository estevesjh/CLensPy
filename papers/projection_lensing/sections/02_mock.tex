\section{The mock and the estimators}
\label{sec:mock}

The reference is the Costanzi mock catalogue
\pyfile{mock\_lob\_sigma\_catalog.fits}: \NHalosTotal{} halos of an
octant light-cone (Buzzard v1.1 cosmology, $\Omega_m = 0.286$,
$h = 0.7$), of which \NHalosProfiled{} carry surface-density profiles.
Each halo is dressed with an analytic NFW profile (Duffy et al.\ 2008
$c_{200m}$, $M_{200m}$ from the $z{=}0$ comoving mean density,
untruncated Baltz $\tau \to \infty$ form) that is Monte-Carlo sampled
into equal-mass particles out to $30\,h^{-1}$cMpc of its centre. A true
richness is drawn from the DES~Y1 HOD
($\log_{10} M_{\rm min} = 11.385$, $\log_{10} M_1 = 12.696$,
$\alpha = 0.859$, $\sigma_\lambda = 0.181$, $\epsilon = 0.284$,
$z_\star = 0.4544$ --- the exact set \modname{HodMor.buzzard()}
carries), and an observed richness by counting photo-z--weighted mock
galaxies through the redMaPPer aperture and radial filter, without
percolation. Per halo, the catalogue's \pyfile{SIGMA\_PRJ} column is the target-removed
mass-map profile $\Stot(R) = \Sbkg + \Sprj$ --- mass per comoving annulus on 20
log bins spanning $0.01$--$30\,h^{-1}$cMpc from all \emph{other} halos
within a sharp comoving top-hat of half-depth $50\,h^{-1}$cMpc,
excluding neighbours inside the 3-D chord ball
$\Rexcl = \Rlam(1+z)$. The full recipe, including the reverse-engineered
differences between the delivered file and the tutorial notebook, is
documented in \pyfile{MOCK\_RECIPE.md} alongside the catalogue.

The model is evaluated in the mock-matched configuration: hard
line-of-sight window of half-depth $50\,h^{-1}$cMpc, ball exclusion,
halo-centric truncation at $30\,h^{-1}$cMpc, transverse aperture
$60\,h^{-1}$cMpc, \modname{HodMor.buzzard()}, and Buzzard~v1.1
cosmology with a CAMB halofit $\xinl$. Model profiles are
annulus-averaged on the mock's radial grid --- the mock's $\Sigma$ is a
mass per annulus, not a midpoint sample --- and the first four
(Poisson-starved) annuli are dropped. The mock is $h$-scaled and the
package $h$-free; radii, masses and surface densities convert once each
at the script boundary.

\paragraph{Estimators.} Two statistics are compared per
$(\lob, z)$ bin, on the grid
$\lob \in [20, 30, 45, 60, 500)$, $z \in [0.20, 0.35, 0.50, 0.65)$:
the \emph{absolute} stacked $\langle\Stot\rangle$, and the
selected-to-random \emph{ratio}
$\langle\Stot\rangle_{\lambda}/\langle\Stot\rangle_{\rm RND}$, where the
random stack reweights the full profiled population to the selected
sample's $(\ln M, z)$ histogram (Hao-Yi Wu's estimator; cells of
$d\ln M = 0.1$, $dz = 0.05$). The model ratio divides the
$\bsel(\theta)$ evaluation by the same model with the constant $\beff$
in its place --- the same masses and redshifts, unbiased clustering
weight. Each bin is evaluated at the number-counts operator's own first
moments, $\langle\lob\rangle_{ij} = N[\lambda]/N[1]$ and
$\langle z\rangle_{ij} = N[z]/N[1]$ --- the model never reads the mock
for its evaluation points. Those predictions agree with the mock's
selected-sample means to better than $1.4\%$ in $\lob$ and $0.005$ in
$z$ in every bin, a further independent check of the counts operator.

One caveat of the catalogue matters for interpretation: the stored
richness columns are a statistically identical but \emph{independent}
re-draw relative to the draw that decided which halos received profiles,
so object-level joint conditioning is not self-consistent; the ensemble
averages used here are unaffected. The mock's observed-richness kernel
is its own (unpercolated) galaxy counting, while the model's closure
uses the DES~Y3 EMG calibration --- a genuine model--mock difference
that surfaces in the inner region (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
