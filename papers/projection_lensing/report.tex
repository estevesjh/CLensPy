       \Large \textbf{$\Sprj$ and $\DSprj$: implementation and validation
       against the Costanzi mock}}
\author{J.\,H.\,Esteves\thanks{ORCID:
  \href{https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4373-2386}{0000-0003-4373-2386}}}
\date{2026-08-28}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We implement the projected two-halo surface density around
richness-selected clusters, $\Sprj(R\,|\,\lob,\zob)$ and its excess
$\DSprj$ (Costanzi et al.\ 2026, Eq.~13), in \modname{CLensPy} as the
exact angular integral of the offset-NFW kernel against the correlated
excess $b\,\bsel(\theta)\,\xinl$ --- the two-halo term carries no
background; the mean matter column is a separate, near-uniform channel
added only when the comparison target is a raw mass map. The effective bias enters as the bin average
$\beff = N[b]/N[1]$ from the number-counts operator, threaded into the
$\bsel$ closure. Validated against the Costanzi mock catalogue
(\NHalosProfiled{} profiled halos), the model reproduces the
selected-to-random stacked ratio in the two-halo regime
($R > 3\,h^{-1}$cMpc) to a maximum absolute deviation of
\MaxTwoHaloResid{} across all \NBins{} $(\lob, z)$ bins, and the absolute
mass-map column $\langle\Stot\rangle$ at $(\lob = 20, \zob = 0.5)$ to
\LegCMaxDev{}. The
closure's parameter-free prediction of the mean projected richness
boost, $\Delta^{\rm prj}_{\rm RND} = P_1 + \beff I_2$, matches the
mock's measured $\langle\lob - \ltr\rangle$ to \DeltaPrjAgreeMin{}--%
\DeltaPrjAgreeMax{} in every bin.
\end{abstract}
