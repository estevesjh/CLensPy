\begin{abstract}
We validate \modname{CLensPy}'s selection-affected halo bias
$\bsel(\theta)$ (\modname{SelBiasEngine}) against two independent
references: Costanzi et al.\ (2026)'s own published Fig.~6 theory curve,
digitized by hand ($\FigSixNPoints$ points, $\FigSixNPanels$
richness/redshift panels), and the Buzzard mock catalogue used to build
that figure. Against the digitized curve, the model's
selected-to-random $\Sprj$ ratio agrees to a median $\FigSixMedianAbsResid$
(worst $\FigSixMaxAbsResid$) --- down from a $\sim$50\% median before a
closure fix derived this session. Against the mock, $\MockNPass$ of
$\MockNBins$ $(\lob,z)$ bins pass in the two-halo regime ($R>3\,\hMpc$,
max residual $\MockTwoHaloMinResid$--$\MockTwoHaloMaxResid$), and the
closure's implied mean true richness $\langle\ltr\rangle$ agrees with the
mock's directly stored value to a median $\MockLtrMedianResid$. We also
report the closure's sensitivity to the HOD mass-richness slope $\alpha$
and to $(\Omega_m,\sigma_8)$: the selected/RND ratio moves by at most
$\CosmoMaxAbsRatioShift$ under a $\pm10\%$ cosmology shift, so the
dominant systematic is the mass-observable relation, not cosmology.
\end{abstract}
