\section{Validation against the Buzzard mock catalogue}
\label{sec:mock}

\textbf{Script:} \pyfile{scripts/make\_mock.py} (reuses
\pyfile{validation/validate\_sigma\_prj\_mock.py}'s production chain).
\textbf{Reference:} \pyfile{mock\_lob\_sigma\_catalog.fits} under
\texttt{\$SELECTION\_BIAS\_DIR} --- 3{,}009{,}025 halos of an octant
light-cone dressed with untruncated NFW profiles and a synthetic
redMaPPer richness, storing both \texttt{LAMBDA\_TR\_LOB} and
\texttt{LAMBDA\_OB\_LOB} per halo, and the target-removed $\Sprj(R)$ on
20 log annuli (recipe: \pyfile{MOCK\_RECIPE.md} in the same directory).
Cosmology: Buzzard v1.1 ($\Omega_m=0.286$, $h=0.7$, $\sigma_8=0.82$,
$n_s=0.96$), confirmed --- not assumed --- via
\pyfile{costanzi\_notebook/cosmology.py}, a verbatim transcription of the
mock-generation notebook's own cosmology cell.

\paragraph{The $\langle\ltr\rangle$ cross-check.} Unscored and reported:
the inner aperture, $R\lesssim2\Rlam$, is Poisson-starved in the mock and
dominated by $\bsmall$, which this comparison does not have the
statistics to score tightly. A tighter, model-agnostic check is available
instead: the closure's own implied mean true richness,
$\langle\ltr\rangle=\lob-\DRND(1+\delta)$, can be compared directly to
the mock's stored \texttt{LAMBDA\_TR\_LOB} mean --- no $\Sprj$ profile, no
digitized figure, and no bias model in the way. It agrees to a median
$\MockLtrMedianResid$ (worst $\MockLtrMaxResid$) across the 6
cross-checked bins, the tightest test in this report, because it probes
the closure's one physical input before the closure's own $\times$18--40
gain $A_s$ (\pyfile{docs/plan-bsel-stable-closure.md}) amplifies whatever
error is in it.
