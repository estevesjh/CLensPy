\section{Sensitivity of $\bsel$ to the HOD slope $\alpha$}
\label{sec:alpha}

\textbf{Script:} \pyfile{scripts/make\_alpha\_sensitivity.py}.
\modname{HodMor.buzzard()}'s mass-richness slope $\alpha=0.859$ is a fit
to DES Y1 NC+3x2pt, not a closed-form number --- how much does it drive
$\bsmall$ and $\blarge$? Scaling $\alpha$ by $\{0.9,1.0,1.1,1.2,1.3,1.5\}$
at fixed $M_{\min}$, $M_1$, $\epsilon$, $\sigma_{\rm intr}$ (recomputing
$\beff$ fresh per variant, since it depends on the MOR too), at the same
6 richness/redshift bins used in \S\ref{sec:fig6}--\S\ref{sec:mock}:

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.85\textwidth]{fig_alpha_sensitivity.pdf}
\caption{$B_{\rm small}$ and $B_{\rm large}$ vs.\ $\alpha$, at the 3
redshift bins (colour) for the two digitized richness bins (solid:
$\lambda[20,30)$, dashed: $\lambda[60,500)$).}
\label{fig:alpha}
\end{figure}

Both plateaus fall monotonically with $\alpha$: a steeper HOD slope
widens the halo-mass range mapped to a fixed richness, pulling in
lower-bias halos on average. Log-log elasticities
$\partial\ln B/\partial\ln\alpha$ over the full grid:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
& $\lambda[20,30)$ & $\lambda[60,500)$ \\
\midrule
$\bsmall$ & $\AlphaLowElastSmallMin$ to $\AlphaLowElastSmallMax$ &
  $\AlphaHighElastSmallMin$ to $\AlphaHighElastSmallMax$ \\
$\blarge$ & $\AlphaLowElastLargeMin$ to $\AlphaLowElastLargeMax$ &
  $\AlphaHighElastLargeMin$ to $\AlphaHighElastLargeMax$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

$\bsmall$ is consistently the more $\alpha$-sensitive of the two, and
both are more sensitive at high richness than at low --- driven mostly by
$\beff$'s own $\alpha$-dependence (which grows the same way with
richness) rather than the closure's excess-richness amplification on top
of it. This systematic therefore largely cancels in a ratio observable
that already divides by $\beff$ (e.g.\ \S\ref{sec:mock}'s selected/RND
ratio), and matters most for anything reporting $\bsmall$/$\blarge$ in
absolute units.
